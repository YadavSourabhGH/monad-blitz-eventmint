## 🎮 **HOW TO DEMO (5 Minutes)**

### **Step 1: Setup** (30 seconds)
1. **Get MON tokens:** https://faucet.monad.xyz/
2. **Add Monad network** to MetaMask:
   - Network: Monad Testnet
   - RPC: https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz
   - Chain ID: 10143
   - Currency: MON

### **Step 2: Connect Wallet** (15 seconds)
1. Open http://localhost:3000
2. Click "🚀 Connect Wallet & Get Started"
3. Approve MetaMask connection

### **Step 3: Create Event** (60 seconds)
1. Click "🎯 Organizer" tab
2. Fill form: Name, location, date, price (0.05 ETH), tickets (100)
3. Click "🚀 Create Event"
4. Approve transaction (2-5 seconds!)

### **Step 4: Buy Ticket** (60 seconds)
1. Click "🎫 Buy Tickets" tab
2. See your event in marketplace
3. Click "🎟️ Buy Ticket"
4. Confirm purchase (NFT minted instantly!)

### **Step 5: View NFT** (45 seconds)
1. Click "🎫 My Tickets" tab
2. See your NFT ticket
3. Click "📱 View QR" - show QR code

### **Step 6: Verify Entry** (90 seconds)
1. Click "📱 Scanner" tab
2. Scan QR code (or enter token ID)
3. Show verification result
4. Click "✅ Grant Entry & Redeem"
5. Ticket marked as used on-chain!

### **Step 7: Blockchain Proof** (30 seconds)
1. Click "🔗 Transactions" tab
2. Show transaction history
3. Click explorer link - verify on Monad blockchain!
